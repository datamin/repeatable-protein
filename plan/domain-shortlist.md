# Domain shortlist

Checked: 2026-09-02

Registry status was checked against the Verisign `.com` RDAP service. A `no record` response is a strong availability signal, not a reservation: a domain can be registered by someone else at any time. A registrar must confirm availability immediately before purchase.

| Rank | Domain | Registry signal | Why it works | Tradeoff |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `repeatableprotein.com` | No registration record found | Exact match to the current brand and method; memorable and descriptive | Narrows the brand to protein, which is currently the intended niche |
| 2 | `repeatableplate.com` | No registration record found | Broader enough for future meals, habits, and strength content | Does not communicate protein as clearly |
| 3 | `proteinbydefault.com` | No registration record found | Distinctive and expresses the “decide once” idea | Less direct connection to the existing Repeatable Protein name |
| 4 | `repeatablefuel.com` | No registration record found | Shorter and flexible beyond meal plans | “Fuel” can sound more sports-focused |
| 5 | `theproteinroutine.com` | No registration record found | Descriptive and aligned with routine-based content | Longer to type and say |

## Recommendation

Use `repeatableprotein.com` if the registrar still shows it as available. It preserves the name already used on the website and makes the promise clear without forcing a keyword-heavy domain.

Avoid `proteinmadepractical.com` as the main brand even though no registration record was found: “Protein Made Practical” is already used as a recurring name in active nutrition media. Also avoid the currently registered `proteinonrepeat.com`, `proteinroutine.com`, and `theproteinmethod.com`.

This research is not legal or trademark clearance. Before investing materially in the brand, run a formal trademark search in the countries where the business will operate.
