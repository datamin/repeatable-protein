## Recommended content sequence from this pass

1. **P001:** How to Eat 100g of Protein a Day Without Tracking Every Meal — primary US demand; include India examples but do not mix the two SERP intents.
2. **P002:** High-Protein Vegetarian Meal Plan — US ingredients and measurements, with the repeatable breakfast/lunch system as differentiation.
3. **P003:** 100g Protein Indian Diet — Indian ingredients, cooked weights, household measures, vegetarian and egg options.
4. **P009:** Protein per 100g of Vegetarian Foods — India reference hub linking to carefully sourced food pages.
