## Keyword-intent observations from Semrush

### US: 100 g per day

- High-volume wording includes “how to get,” “how to eat,” “what does it look like,” and “a day.”
- A visual, example-led guide should satisfy the cluster better than a protein-definition article.
- “Without tracking” is best treated as the differentiating mechanism until its own volume is validated.

### India: 100 g ambiguity

- The broad 100g-protein result set is dominated by food-composition queries such as paneer, soya chunks, tofu, chana, rajma, and curd per 100 g.
- “100g protein diet” is the clearer daily-target query.
- Do not mix food-reference and daily-meal-plan intent on the same URL.

### Vegetarian meal plan

- The broader “high protein vegetarian meal plan” phrase has measurable demand; the exact “100g protein vegetarian” seed did not produce a useful US keyword table.
- Lead the title/H1 with the broader phrase and present 100 g as a concrete version of the plan.
